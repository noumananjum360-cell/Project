PRIVACY NOTICE - {{STORE_NAME}} loyalty programme

What we collect
- Your name and phone number (email optional), your purchases and returns at our store, the dates you visit,
  and short service notes (for example sizes or preferences).

Why
- To recognise you when you show your loyalty card, QR code or phone number, to keep your purchase history,
  to give loyalty and VIP benefits, and - only if you agree separately - to send you offers.

What we do NOT do
- We do not identify you with cameras or face recognition. If this ever changes, we will ask for your separate,
  written consent first, and you can say no without losing any benefit.
- We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it
- Your profile is kept while you are a member. Visit records and inactive profiles are deleted or anonymised
  after the periods set by the store. Sales records are kept as required for the store's accounts, without
  your personal details once you leave the programme.

Your rights
- Ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, withdraw consent for messages, or ask us to delete your
  details at any time - speak to the store manager.
